## 2026-04-18 - Semantic Level Selection
**Learning:** Using a `<nav>` with `aria-label` for level selection improves screen reader navigation by clearly identifying the navigation landmark and its purpose.
**Action:** Always wrap menu-like button groups in semantic navigation elements with descriptive labels.

## 2026-05-15 - Interactive Level Selection Feedback
**Learning:** Adding difficulty-based colors and hover/focus transitions to level selection buttons significantly improves the "game-like" feel and helps users quickly identify their desired challenge level.
**Action:** Use distinct, themed colors for difficulty levels and always provide visual scaling/brightness feedback on interaction.
